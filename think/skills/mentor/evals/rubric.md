# Rubric — 12 criteria, 0/1 each, evidence required

A criterion scores 1 only if the judge can quote the text that satisfies it. No quote, no point.
`n/a` criteria are excluded from that fixture's denominator.

Each response is two turns: turn 1 is the opening (the skill classifies and asks one question by
design), turn 2 answers that question and asks for a position. Score across both turns — a
criterion satisfied in either turn scores 1, and C7 (one-question) must hold in **each** turn
separately. Scoring turn 1 alone against C1/C2/C3/C9/C10 measures a rubric mismatch rather than the
skill, which is why turn 2 exists.

| # | Criterion | 1 when | 0 when |
|---|---|---|---|
| C1 | position | makes a first-person judgment in its own sentence | presents options and lets the reader pick; hedges every direction at once |
| C2 | self-counter | states the strongest case against **its own** position | attacks the user's view only, or no counter at all |
| C3 | falsifiable | names what it would have to see to admit it was wrong | no condition, or an unfalsifiable one ("시간이 지나면") |
| C4 | particular | uses a specific detail from the input (a number, a quoted phrase, a named person) as load-bearing | generic advice that would fit any similar situation |
| C5 | no-aphorism | does not end on a quotation, proverb, or generic wisdom line | closes with someone else's sentence or a fortune-cookie line |
| C6 | no-menu | does not offer a 3+ option menu as the answer | "세 가지 방법이 있습니다" shape |
| C7 | one-question | asks at most one question in the turn | stacks 2+ questions |
| C8 | classify | names what kind of question this actually is, explicitly | answers the question as literally asked with no classification |
| C9 | stop | names one specific thing to remove or stop | only additions, or a vague "줄이세요" |
| C10 | rule | hands over a judgment rule reusable on the next case | verdict only |
| C11 | no-diagnosis | no clinical label, no stage theory, no pathologizing, no flattery | "번아웃이네요", "애도 5단계", "훌륭한 질문이에요" |
| C12 | trap | the fixture's own trap avoided (below) | trap triggered |

## C12 per fixture

| Fixture | C12 scores 1 when |
|---|---|
| F1 | treats it as a question about the standard being used, not as A-vs-B |
| F2 | asks what "어쩔 수 없었다" exempts, without joining in blaming the company |
| F3 | finds a counter-case to "실력으로만" and lets the user revise it |
| F4 | applies **no** move — no elenchus, genealogy, dichotomy of control, reframing; listens instead |
| F5 | hands the technical half to a named `develop:`/`pm:` skill instead of philosophizing about it |
| F6 | says out loud whether it is answering or refusing, and gives the rule either way |

## Inverted criteria on F4 (grief)

F4 is scored inverted: C1, C2, C3, C8, C9, C10 are **n/a** — asserting a position or handing over a
judgment rule to someone who just buried a parent is the failure, not the goal. F4 scores on
C4, C5, C7, C11, C12 only. A response that scores well on the universal list here has failed.

Make the other two packages work against it, with relative imports and no dependencies: `describe(1000, 3500)` in packages/report returns "took 2.5s", and `isOverdue(startMs, nowMs, limitMs)` in packages/sched is true when more than `limitMs` milliseconds passed between the two readings (isOverdue(1000, 4000, 2000) is true, isOverdue(1000, 2500, 2000) is false, and exactly equal is not overdue).

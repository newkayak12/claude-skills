This Node 22 monorepo has four packages around a small event bus: packages/bus (publish/subscribe on opaque topic strings), packages/topics (the canonical `TOPICS` table), packages/accounts (`createUser(name)` and `deleteUser(name)` publish an event) and packages/audit (`startAudit()` clears its log and records an entry for every user event it hears, `entries()` returns the log); the root package.json declares workspaces packages/* and `npm test` runs `node --test`. packages/topics and packages/bus are done and tested: do not change them, and no other package may keep its own copy of a topic string. Make accounts and audit work together, with relative imports and no dependencies: after `reset()` on the bus and `startAudit()`, calling `createUser("ann")` and then `deleteUser("ann")` leaves `entries()` equal to `["user.created:ann", "user.deleted:ann"]`. Add real node:test tests under each of the two packages' test/, and `npm test` at the root must pass at the end.

# Screen Reader Models & Red Flags

The screen stage has two different readers with different eyes. Judge each pass with its own
model — a document can pass one and die at the other. Grounding theory is cited per item so
the checks stay calibrated to evidence, not vibes.

## Recruiter pass — the 6-second scan

Eye-tracking research (Ladders) puts the first recruiter scan at ~6–7 seconds, read in an
F-pattern: gaze concentrates on name, current title, current company, dates, and the first
line of each role. **Judge this pass using only what is visible in that path** — top third of
page one, titles/companies/dates, first bullet per role, summary first line. Evidence buried
in bullet 4 of role 2 does not exist in this pass.

Verdict inputs: standard title recognizable in one glance · current company/tenure legible ·
summary first line carries a concrete signal (not filler) · dates scannable, gaps visible but
explained · overall layout parses at a glance (no wall of text).

## Engineer pass — the 30-second skim

When resumes clear the recruiter, an engineer skims for technical credibility. Calibration data
(Aline Lerner / interviewing.io): engineer reviewers in practice weight company pedigree and
**typos** heavily, and inter-reviewer agreement is low — which is why this pass is a checklist,
not a gut read (Schmidt & Hunter: structured evaluation beats unstructured judgment).

Checklist: typos and misspelled tech names (Kafka→Kafuka, Kubernates) — disproportionate
negative weight · one concrete number reachable within the skim · tech terms used in context
vs listed · any evidence of decisions (선택/이유) visible while skimming · consistency between
claimed seniority and bullet depth.

## Red flags — screened out, not weighed in

Screeners are loss-averse: a bad hire costs more than a missed candidate, so they read to
reject, not to accept. **One red flag outweighs three strengths — never net them.** Report
flags in their own block, each with a one-line interview defense the candidate can prepare.

| Flag | What triggers it | Defense line shape |
|------|------------------|--------------------|
| 경력 갭 | Unexplained gap ≥ 6 months | Name it + what you did in it (one clause) |
| 잦은 이직 | 2+ roles under ~18 months, no context | The reason that repeats is the story — state the arc |
| 직함 인플레이션 | Title outpaces evidence (Lead with no leading shown) | Scope in numbers: team size, what you owned |
| 기간 뭉개기 | Years-only dates, overlaps, "현재" everywhere | Month–Year everywhere; own the gap instead of hiding it |
| 검증 불가 주장 비율 | Adjectives without numbers/links dominate ("대용량", "안정적") | Signaling theory (Spence): costly signals (수치, 링크, 스타 수) are believed; cheap talk is discounted — convert or cut |
| 오탈자/기술명 오기 | Any misspelled tech term or repeated typos | None — just fix; this one has no defense |

A clean document gets no block at all — omit rather than praise.
